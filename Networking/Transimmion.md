# Data Transmission

Data transmission carries information between devices through a communication medium.

## Analog and digital

- **Analog information** is represented by continuously varying quantities.
- **Digital information** is represented by discrete values, typically binary data.

Digital data can also be carried by modulating an analog carrier signal. Wireless systems use modulation to carry information over radio links.

## Transmission modes

| Mode | Direction of communication |
| --- | --- |
| Simplex | Communication goes in one direction only. |
| Half-duplex | Both sides can transmit, but not simultaneously. |
| Full-duplex | Both sides can transmit and receive simultaneously. |

Modern switched Ethernet connections normally operate in full-duplex mode.

## Transmission media

**Wired media** include:

- Twisted-pair copper cables.
- Coaxial cables.
- Fiber-optic cables.

Copper cables carry electrical signals, while optical fiber carries light.

**Wireless communication** uses signals such as radio waves, including those used by Wi-Fi. Infrared is also used for certain short-range applications.

The choice of medium depends on distance, required bandwidth, interference, installation cost, and the environment in which it will operate.
